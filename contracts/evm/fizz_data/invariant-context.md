## Protocol in one paragraph

USDC-only L1 escrow for an Aztec bridge. `TokenPortal` (src/TokenPortal.sol, 154 nSLOC) locks USDC against Aztec L1→L2 Inbox messages (`depositToAztecPublic/Private`, and router-only `...For` variants that name the Permit2 signer as depositor) and pays out against proven L2→L1 Outbox messages (`withdraw`). It keeps NO internal ledger: every movement is checked against its own token-balance delta. `Permit2DepositRouter` (src/Permit2DepositRouter.sol, 87 nSLOC) pulls a Permit2 witness transfer from `msg.sender` (the signer) and forwards it to the portal's `...For` deposit naming that signer. Both contracts are ownerless: no setters, no pause, no sweep, no upgrade. `initialize` is initializer-only (immutable `initializer` = deployer) and one-shot (`registry` zero → non-zero).
